KOHJEECHA is a home-based matcha cafe at Lakeside Gardens, Singapore.

The public menu combines haru-cha matcha drinks, cloud-foam creations, koji-cha/hojicha variants and small-batch butter tteok.

The visual world is intentionally nocturnal: charcoal, matcha green, cream and a restrained taro-purple accent.

Tea is the
main character.
