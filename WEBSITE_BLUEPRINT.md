## Site map

## Visual

## Motion/VFX
Liquid matcha swirl in hero; night-glow particles; drink ingredients slide into layered compositions; menu cards tilt slightly; scroll reveals use blur/opacity/y; section exits reverse cleanly; subtle cursor glow desktop only.

## Functional
Live schedule, preorder link, sold-out labels, butter-tteok preorder rules, ingredient/allergen notes, pickup instructions, Instagram/DM CTA once verified, local SEO.

## Do not invent
Current phone, WhatsApp, social handles, payment method, halal status or exact current hours beyond the live source/owner confirmation.
